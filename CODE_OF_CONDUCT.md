# Community Code of Conduct

Project Seed welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific in technical disagreement.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish another person's private data.
- Credit others' work and distinguish evidence from speculation.
- Help new contributors understand the project and its safety boundaries.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, and other project-run
spaces. Maintainers may edit/remove harmful material, close discussions, or
restrict participation proportionately. Enforcement should be explained without
exposing private information, and conflicts of interest should be disclosed.

For ordinary concerns, ask for maintainer moderation without posting sensitive
details. For abuse involving private information or personal safety, use GitHub's
report-abuse mechanisms. The project has not yet established a dedicated private
community-reporting address; do not assume public issues are confidential.
